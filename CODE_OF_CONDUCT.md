# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
As contributors and maintainers of this project we pledge to make participation in our project and community a diverse, welcome, and inclusive environment. This means fostering a harassment-free experience for everyone regardless of ethnicity, age, body size, nationality, race, religion, appearance, gender identity and expression, sexual identity and orientation. 

## Our responsibilities and expected behaviour
As a contributors and maintainers of this project we have a responsbility to maintain a diverse, welcome and inclusive community. As such it is our responsibility to:

* Use welcoming and inclusive language
* Approaching differing viewpoints and experiences with respect and patience
* Providing constructive criticism in a respectful and patient manner
* Accepting constructive criticism in a respectful and graceful manner
* Showing empathy for community members


Examples of unacceptable behaviors include:

* Sexualized language, imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
* Harassment of any kind
* Publishing private information, communication, and messages of others without explicit permission and/or consent
* Witch hunting
* Trolling, insulting, making derogatory personal or political attacks
* Any conduct not considered appropriate in a professional setting

## Where the code of conduct is applied
The responsibilities and expected behaviours listed in this Code of Conduct are expected to be followed within community and project spaces, however this also extends to any spaces where an individual is representing said community and/or project. Representations includes but is not limited to using official project e-mail addresses, official project social media accounts and channels, or in any online or offline project representative role. Further details on what is defined as representation of the project can be explained and clarified by project maintainers, so please feel free to reachout.

## Enforcement
Violation of any of the above guidelines such as instances of abuse, harassment, or unacceptable behaviour should be reported to the project team at barrysiu@student.ubc.ca. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated by the team, the team will also ensure the confidentiality of the reporter of the incident be maintained. Anyone found violating the Code of Conduct (both maintainers or contributors) may face temporary or permanent repercussions.
